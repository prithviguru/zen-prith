with timestamps
A day holds as many sessions as you like, each with its own time, rating and length, and the calendar shows a dot for each. Your weekly goal still counts days, so two sessions on Monday don't cover Tuesday.

Welcome to Zen
A proper welcome for anyone new
Opening Zen for the first time now takes three quick screens — what it is, your name, and how often you want to practice. Already set up? You won't see it. Pass the link to someone who isn't, and they will.

Not on your home screen yet? Open prithviguru.github.io/zen-prith in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
